## Exports and your own projects

Exported HTML includes the MIT code notice and this asset notice. The MIT notice must remain with copies or substantial portions of the code. It does not require keeping the visible “Made with Motion Lab” link.

Use your own images and branding in your projects, or comply with the applicable sample-image license. Exports made with the Sted preset include its case assets; replace those before reusing the design for your own product. Images you select locally are processed in your browser and are never uploaded by Motion Lab. Their inclusion in an export does not change their ownership or license.

Sted is an example case. Motion Lab is an independent project by Dante Galeazzi.
